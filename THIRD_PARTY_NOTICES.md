# Third-party research and dependencies

MuNote source in this repository is a clean-room implementation.

Research references:
- xnotes-android (MIT): https://github.com/shardulvs/xnotes-android
- Saber (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/saber-notes/saber
- PicPocket (MIT): https://github.com/pipolarbear/picpocket
- PDF_Scanner (license not established at research time): https://github.com/meuse24/PDF_Scanner

No source code from Notein or TouchNotes/享做笔记 is included. They are proprietary products and are referenced only for observed product behavior and interaction goals.

Runtime libraries retain their own licenses. In particular MuNote currently uses AndroidX/Jetpack Compose, Kotlin coroutines/serialization, and Google ML Kit text recognition.
